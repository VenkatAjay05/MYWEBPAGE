APJ Abdul Kalam, born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, was an Indian aerospace engineer and the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. He was known as the "Missile Man of India" for his significant contributions to India's defense and space programs, including the development of the Agni and Prithvi missiles. Kalam was awarded the Bharat Ratna for his service to the nation and was recognized for his efforts in promoting education and technology. He passed away on July 27, 2015, leaving behind a legacy of inspiration and dedication to India's scientific and technological advancemen
